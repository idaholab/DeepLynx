# Member Management

The Users tab of the Project Management page has a table containing all active and pending members.
**Project admins** have the ability to add, edit, and remove users and groups from projects.

## Adding Members to Projects

<Steps>
### From the landing page, select the "Explore" button of the desired project within the "You Projects" modal.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/nav-proj-management.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

### Once on the Project Management page, navigate to the "Project Settings" section by clicking the slider bar icon on the inner toolbar.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/proj-settings.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

### On the Users tab of the Project Management page, click the "Add Group" or "Add User" button.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/add-member-proj.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

### Select the members and their roles then click "Add to Project".

    - For Groups: select an existing group and role.
    - For Users: select the user's email address and role.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/add-group-proj.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

<Image
  src="/docs/images/add-user-proj.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

### The new members will appear in the members table of the Project Management page!

</Steps>

## Editing Members in Projects

<Steps>
### Click the pencil icon in the actions column of the member management table.
### Update the member's information in the Edit Member modal and hit save.

## Removing Members from Projects
<Steps>
### Click the trashcan icon in the action column of the member management table.
### This will remove the member from the project.

**Note:** Once a member is removed from the project, they must be readded to regain access.
