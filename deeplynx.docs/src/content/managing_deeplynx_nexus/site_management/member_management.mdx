# Member Management

The Member Management tab of the Site Management page has a table containing all active and pending members.
**Site admins** can invite users, edit their details, and remove users from Nexus.
All users are placed in the INL organization as a default, but can be added to other organizations.

## Inviting Users

<Steps>
### Navigate to the Member Management page by clicking the cog icon on the outtermost toolbar.
### On the Member Management tab of the Site Management page, click the "Invite User" button.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/invite-site.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>

### Enter the user's email address and hit "Send Invitation".

### The user will receive an email containing a link to join Nexus!

**Note:** Once a user accepts an email invitation their status will be switched to "Active".

<Image
  src="/docs/images/invite-save-site.png"
  alt="Invite User UI"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>
</Steps>

## Editing Users

<Steps>
### Click the pencil icon in the action column of the member management table.
### Update the member's information in the Edit User modal and hit save.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/edit-user-site.png"
  alt="Edit User UI"
  width={800}
  height={400}
/>
</Steps>

## Removing Users

<Steps>
### Click the trashcan icon in the action column of the member management table.
### This will remove the user from Nexus.

**Note:** Once a user is removed from the organization, a new invitation is required to regain access.

<Image
  src="/docs/images/delete-user-site.png"
  alt="Delete User UI"
  width={800}
  height={400}
/>
</Steps>
